Who we are

A community, not a committee

Women in Power started in 2024 as a handful of women in the Power Platform community who were tired of being the only one in the room — and decided to do something about it rather than wait.

Today we are several thousand strong. What we do is simple: we help women in this ecosystem grow, learn and connect. That happens through three things: mentoring, skilling and networking.

What we do

Three pillars

Mentoring

Twelve-week cohorts in two tiers — group mentoring for women new to the programme, one-to-one pairings for those coming back. Volunteer-led, and running now.

Skilling Series

Monthly community sessions on Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Dataverse, AI Builder and Copilot. All levels, no experience assumed — just show up ready to build.

Networking

What we believe

More women in the room, growing, learning and connecting

Nobody grows in a vacuum

Careers move through people. The fastest way to help a woman advance is to introduce her to someone who can open a door — and then make sure she knows how to ask.

Everyone gets to speak

In most rooms the same few voices carry the conversation. Our programmes are built so that every participant leaves having spoken in front of the group at least once.

Skills, not just encouragement

Confidence follows competence. Alongside the community side we run real technical skilling, because “believe in yourself” is not a certification path.

Built by volunteers

Every mentor, host and organiser here does this on their own time, around a full-time job. It is why the programmes cost nothing, and why we take people’s hours seriously.

Part of something bigger

Women’s Innovation Network

Women in Power is a programme of the Women’s Innovation Network (WIN), a non-profit set up to get more women to conferences, into workshops, and through certifications — and to build the support around them that makes those things possible.

Power Platform is where we started. WIN is how we keep going as the technology changes.

Get involved

There is no membership form

You are in as soon as you turn up to something.

  1. Follow us on LinkedIn. Every skilling session, cohort registration and event is announced there first.
  2. Come to a Skilling Series session. Monthly, open to all, no experience assumed. The easiest way to meet people here.
  3. Join a mentoring cohort — as a mentee or a mentor. Registration opens ahead of each cohort.
  4. Then bring someone with you. That is genuinely the whole model.
