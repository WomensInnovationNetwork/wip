Why bother

A conference is a lonely place on your own

Two hundred sessions, twenty thousand square feet of expo, and a lot of rooms where you might be the only one who looks like you. That is a very different week when you walk in with people.

Women in Power is a volunteer-led community across the Microsoft Power Platform and AI ecosystem, built on three things: mentoring, skilling and networking. PPCC is where the whole community lands in one place, so we have put something on every day of it — and we have designed all of it around meeting people rather than sitting in rows.

The program

Three days, four ways in

Everything below is listed on the PPCC website and in the conference app, so you can add it to your schedule now and not think about it again.

01 Tuesday 27 October Connection day

12:30 – 2:00 PM

Women in Power Networking Luncheon

Dedicated Women in Power lunch hall · seats 600

No stage program this year. The whole thing is built around meeting the women you will spend the rest of the week with.

  • Round tables of ten, with icebreakers and a grounding topic on every table
  • Find your conference buddy — leave lunch with someone to sit with, walk with and cheer for over the next two days
  • A guided LinkedIn moment, so you leave with the connections actually made rather than intended
  • A spotlight on the women speaking at PPCC, so you can plan which sessions to catch

Open to everyone · add it in the app

Evening · during the Night Market, 5:00 – 8:00 PM

Night Market Meetup

Expo Hall and Makers Hall · meeting point announced closer to the event

Walking into a party alone is the worst part of any conference. Meet us first and walk in together — drinks, the expo floor and the makers community, with a group you already know.

02 Wednesday 28 October Our headline

2:00 PM PT · 60 minutes

The Women in Power Panel

Featured session · ballroom, full AV

A moderated conversation with women leading across product, engineering and the customer community: how the platform is actually changing, and what it takes to lead and influence while it does.

  • Sits in the PPCC content catalog — add it to your schedule early
  • Last year we hit fire code capacity with women sitting on the floor. This year we moved to a much bigger room
  • Full panelist lineup announced soon

03 Thursday 29 October Practical

60 minutes · time announced soon

Roundtable: Your Network Is Your Net Worth

Small group roundtable · listed in the content catalog

No slides. A small room, real talk, and practical guidance you can act on before you fly home — growing a network, showing up on LinkedIn, and building the professional brand that opens doors.

To the allies reading this

Yes, this means you.

Every Women in Power activation at PPCC is open to every attendee. Not tolerated — wanted. The rooms where things change are the ones with allies in them, and the fastest way to be a good one is to turn up, listen, and introduce someone to somebody who can open a door.

Bring a colleague who has never been to one of these. That is the single most useful thing you can do all week.
