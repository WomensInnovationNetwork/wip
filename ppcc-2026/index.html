Why bother

A conference is a lonely place on your own

Two hundred sessions, twenty thousand square feet of expo, and rooms where you might be the only one who looks like you. It’s a different week when you walk in with people.

Women in Power is a volunteer-led community across the Microsoft Power Platform and AI ecosystem. PPCC is where we all land in one place, so we have something every day, all built around meeting people rather than sitting in rows.

The program

Three days, four ways in

Everything below is on the PPCC website and in the conference app. Add it to your schedule now.

01 Tuesday 27 October Connection day

12:30 – 2:00 PM

Women in Power Networking Luncheon

Dedicated Women in Power lunch hall · seats 600

No stage program this year. It’s all about meeting the women you’ll spend the week with.

  • Round tables of ten, with icebreakers and a grounding topic on every table
  • Find your conference buddy to sit with, walk with and cheer for all week
  • A guided LinkedIn moment, so connections get made, not just intended
  • A spotlight on women speaking at PPCC, to help plan your sessions

Open to everyone · add it in the app

Evening · during the Night Market, 5:00 – 8:00 PM

Night Market Meetup

Expo Hall and Makers Hall · meeting point announced closer to the event

Walking into a party alone is the worst. Meet us first and walk in together: drinks, the expo floor and the makers community.

02 Wednesday 28 October Our headline

2:00 PM PT · 60 minutes

The Women in Power Panel

Featured session · ballroom, full AV

A moderated conversation with women leading across product, engineering and the community: how the platform is changing, and what it takes to lead while it does.

  • Sits in the PPCC content catalog — add it to your schedule early
  • Last year we hit fire-code capacity, with women sitting on the floor. This year we have a much bigger room
  • Full panelist lineup announced soon

Fills up · schedule it now

03 Thursday 29 October Practical

60 minutes · time announced soon

Roundtable: Your Network Is Your Net Worth

Small group roundtable · listed in the content catalog

No slides. A small room, real talk, and practical steps before you fly home: growing your network, showing up on LinkedIn, and building a brand that opens doors.

  • Built for questions, not presenting
  • Come with the thing you have been putting off

To the allies reading this

Yes, this means you.
