About us

Somebody had to start it.

Women in Power is run by women who do it around full-time jobs, because being the only one in the room got old. These are the people behind it — and the community that has shown up.

How this started

A group chat that got out of hand

In 2024 a few women in the Microsoft Power Platform community got tired of waiting for someone else to build the thing they needed. So they built it.

What began as mentoring for a handful of people has become a community around six thousand strong, running cohorts, monthly skilling sessions and a presence at the conferences where this ecosystem gathers. In 2026 it grew again, into the Women’s Innovation Network — a non-profit set up so the support can outlast any one platform or product name.

Nobody here is paid. Every session, every pairing and every certificate is volunteer work, done in evenings and lunch breaks.
