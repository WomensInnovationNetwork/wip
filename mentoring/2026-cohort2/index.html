Which tier am I in?

It depends on your history with this programme — not on how senior you are.

Tier 1

Group mentoring

You’re here if this is your first Women in Power cohort — as a mentor or a mentee, whatever your years in the industry.

Sessions are levelled foundational and advanced, so twenty years of experience is not a reason to skip it. A lot of our strongest room hosts started here.

Tier 2

1:1 mentoring

You’re here if you’ve completed a previous cohort in either tier, or you are a mentor with prior mentoring experience in the programme.

Tier 1 graduates who opted in on their final feedback form are in this tier. Mentors not matched to a 1:1 pair join Tier 1 as room hosts — that is a full role, not a consolation prize.

Both tiers share a spine

One kickoff, one closing call, joint presentations on alternating weeks, the same feedback forms and the same skilling events. You will see the other tier regularly — that is deliberate.

How each tier works

The format, before the detail

Tier 1

Group mentoring

Six bi-weekly one-hour sessions. Each opens with a five to ten minute framing, then splits into breakout rooms by topic. Mentors take a room each, empty rooms are closed, and you choose where to go.

Topics are published a week ahead so you know what you are walking into — the single most requested change from the last cohort. Every room is labelled foundational or advanced.

Breakout topics

  • Power Platform technical
  • Early career navigation
  • Communication confidence
  • Certifications
  • Career pivots & role transitions
  • Getting ready for 1:1 · sessions 5–6

Career pivots was both the most popular breakout topic last cohort and a recent skilling theme. Expect it to be busy.

Tier 2

1:1 mentoring

You are matched with one mentor for the whole cohort and meet on your own cadence — bi-weekly works for most pairs — supported by mentor meetups, joint presentations and a graduation presentation to work toward.

Pairing uses years of experience, goals and time zone from your interest form. Pairing emails go out the week of 14 September, with a few days to request a re-match. Ask early rather than quietly disengaging.

What pairs actually work on

  • Career growth
  • Technical upskilling
  • Résumé & mock interviews
  • Communication & presentation
  • Personal branding
  • Imposter syndrome

The one thing that is not optional

Every 1:1 mentee presents before graduating

Five to ten minutes. A win, a project, a lesson learned, or something you have figured out in the Power Platform. It runs in the joint presentation slot, so both tiers are watching — and you will have rehearsed it with your mentor first.

We added this because in every cohort the same handful of women speak and everyone else stays quiet. The point is not a polished keynote. The point is that you leave this cohort having spoken in front of the group, once, with people who wanted you to do well.

Prep starts in November

Weeks of 16 and 30 Nov, with your mentor. You are not doing this alone.

Dry run first

Full rehearsal with your mentor, and you can take it to a mentor meetup too.

Or co-present

Pairs can present together. If going solo feels like too much, this is the answer.

Questions

Things people ask

What if I miss a session?

Nothing breaks. Certificates use an attendance threshold, not perfect attendance. In Tier 2, tell your mentor rather than going quiet — that is the only thing that genuinely derails a pairing.

My 1:1 pairing does not feel right. Can I change it?

Yes. There is a re-match window in the few days after pairing emails go out the week of 14 September. Ask early; it is much easier then than in November.

The session times do not work for my time zone.

We are alternating group session times across zones this cohort so everyone can attend at least half of them live. For 1:1s, you and your mentor set your own time — time zone is one of the inputs to the matching.

I signed up but I am not getting the emails.

Check your junk folder first — a lot of programme mail landed there last cohort. Your confirmation email lists the exact senders to add to your contacts. Do that, and everything after it will arrive.

I’m in Tier 1. Can I still get 1:1 time with a mentor?

Yes — coffee chats. Any mentor on the Tier 1 list is there because they said yes to being asked. Reach out directly, any time in the cohort.

Do I really have to present?

If you are a Tier 2 mentee, yes. Five minutes counts, you can co-present with your mentor, and you will have done a full dry run first. It is the one requirement we are firm on, and it is the thing graduates most often say mattered.

Can I attend the other tier’s sessions?

The joint presentations and the Skilling Series are open to everyone in the cohort, and that is where the two tiers deliberately overlap. Group sessions and 1:1s stay within their tier.

What happens after 16 December?

Tier 1 graduates can opt into Tier 2 for the next cohort on the final feedback form. Tier 2 graduates are invited into the alumni community and can come back as a mentor, a committee volunteer or a community speaker. Most of our mentors came up this way.
